# Afterglow patch notes

## Oct 19, 2026 · 12:00 UTC

- **The Daily Run.** One hand-picked run per day, the same for every pilot: a fixed ship (even one you have not unlocked yet), one boon and two banes, and no Forge upgrades. Your first flight each day pays a Daily Bounty, keeping a streak raises it, and a log tracks your best score for every day.
- **Eleven mutators.** Volatile enemies that detonate, Lucky Star, Overcharged, Gravity, Gilded elite hunts, Glass Heart, Horde, Frenzy, Giants, Bloodlight and Short Night. Each bane raises the score multiplier, and each one has its own neon icon.
- **Into the Afterdark.** Defeat the Eclipse and you can now fly on. The arena bleeds crimson, the swarm keeps growing, and every 90 seconds the Warden or the Eclipse returns, stronger each night. Your dawn is banked the moment you choose; everything after it is score.
- **Score.** Every run now has a score built from kills, elites, bosses, time and the dawn. It ticks up live in the corner, counts up on the results screen and records your best overall and with every ship, shown in the Hangar.
- **The Codex and five new missions.** A new Codex screen shows all six evolutions (silhouettes until you discover them, recipes always visible) and a field guide to the swarm. New missions: Daybreaker, Ritual, Luminary, Into the Afterdark and Nightmare.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 09:00 UTC

- **The Hangar: seven ships.** Lumen is joined by Vesper the bulwark, Kestrel the hunter, Strix the glass cannon, Halcyon the harvester, Aurora the prism and Nyx the eclipse-born. Each one starts with its own weapon, plays by its own rules and has its own neon silhouette.
- **Overdrive.** Kills charge a ring in the corner of the screen. When it blazes, tap it to unleash your ship's signature power: Flare, Aegis, Barrage, Tempest, Black Sun, Prism Storm or Eclipse, which stops time itself. Space or E also work on a keyboard.
- **Missions.** Sixteen goals to chase, from surviving two minutes to reaching the dawn with three different ships. Every mission pays Flux, and six of them unlock new ships. Records from your earlier runs already count.
- **Nyx, the hard mode.** Fly Nyx and the swarm gets 40% tougher, but you earn 50% more experience and Flux. Reaching the dawn with Nyx pays the biggest bounty in the game.
- **A richer results screen.** Completed missions and newly unlocked ships now appear when a run ends, with a shortcut straight to the Hangar.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 06:00 UTC

- **Weapon Evolutions.** Max out a weapon, carry its catalyst system, then open a Prism Cache to evolve it into Supernova Cannon, Event Horizon, Storm Crown, Singularity, Hydra Swarm or Spectrum. Level-up cards show what evolves with what, and a ready weapon pulses gold in your loadout.
- **Prism Caches.** Elites and bosses now drop glowing caches under a pillar of light, with an arrow on the screen edge when one is off-screen. Opening one stops time for a slot-machine reveal of upgrades, evolutions and Flux.
- **Elite hunts.** Elites now roam in every minute or so after the third minute, so there is always a cache worth chasing.
- **Bosses fight back.** The Warden and the Eclipse are tougher, and they overload if you stall them for too long.
- **A fiercer late game.** The swarm grows tougher and denser after the fifth minute to match your new firepower. Your evolutions are recorded on the title screen and in the results.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 04:00 UTC

- **Welcome to Afterglow.** A brand-new game: pilot a ship of light through a neon arena while the swarm closes in. Drag anywhere to move; your weapons fire on their own.
- **Six weapons, ten systems.** Pulse Cannon, Halo Blades, Arc Coil, Nova Ring, Seeker Swarm and Prism Lance, each with five levels. Pick one of three upgrade cards every time you level up.
- **Eight enemy types and two bosses.** Dashing darts, splitting pentagons, orb-spitting stars, fused bombers, elites with golden halos, the Warden at 4:00 and the Eclipse at 8:00. Defeat the Eclipse to reach the dawn.
- **The Forge.** Every run earns Flux. Spend it on permanent upgrades: hull, power, engine, reroll cards and a once-per-run revive.
- **Built to glow.** Real bloom, a grid that ripples under every explosion, screen shake, slow motion on big kills, haptics and an adaptive synthwave soundtrack that heats up with the fight.

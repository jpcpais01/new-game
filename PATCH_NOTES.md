## Oct 19, 2026 · 03:00 UTC

- **Prisma is here!** Drag glowing gem pieces onto the 8×8 board and fill rows or columns to shatter them into light.
- **Combos and Prism lines.** Keep clearing within 3 moves to grow your multiplier up to ×10, and clear a line of one single colour for a huge PRISM bonus.
- **Daily Prism.** One shared puzzle a day for everyone, with its own best score and a daily streak.
- **Feel every move.** Crystal sounds that climb the scale as your combo grows, an ambient soundtrack, haptics, screen shake, and a sky that lights up while you chain clears.
- **Never lose a run.** Your game saves after every move and continues right where you left off; stats track your best combo, prisms and perfect clears.

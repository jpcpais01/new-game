# Afterglow patch notes

## Oct 20, 2026 · 13:00 UTC

- **Sectors: two new arenas.** Pick where you fly in the Hangar. Each sector has its own sky, its own hazard and its own swarm, and pays a bigger score and more Flux. The Neon Grid is the arena you know.
- **The Abyss.** A deep indigo sea where black holes open and drift after you, bending the grid around them like lenses. They drag in the swarm, your loose gems and you. Whatever reaches a core is crushed (and counts as your kill), and when a black hole collapses it bursts with every bit of light it ate. Feed one 150 of the swarm and it leaves a Prism Cache. Keep clear of the core: it burns. Score x1.2, Flux x1.15, unlocked by defeating the Warden.
- **Corona.** An ember sky around a dying sun that burns at the heart of the arena and blocks your way. Every few seconds it swells and erupts: a wall of fire races outwards with three gaps in it, shown as cool lanes while the sun builds. Be inside a lane when the front reaches you. Everything else burns, the swarm included, and from 4:00 the gaps turn as the fire flies. Score x1.35, Flux x1.25, unlocked by reaching the dawn.
- **Three new missions.** Sated (feed 150 to one black hole), Sunrunner (slip through 15 flares in one run) and Cartographer (reach the dawn in all three sectors), worth 1,400 Flux together. Each sector keeps its own best time and score.
- **Everywhere else.** The title screen's demo now flies through all three sectors, the Codex describes them, run intros and the pause menu name the sector, and new sounds rumble as black holes open and collapse and the sun erupts.

## Oct 20, 2026 · 10:00 UTC

- **New mode: the Gauntlet.** Every boss, back to back. Draft five upgrade cards, then the Leviathan, the Warden, the Seraph and the Eclipse come for you one after another, each 16 seconds after the last one falls, in an arena that burns amber and gold. Clear all four and the cycle starts again, stronger, until you fall. Your ship and Forge fly with you; there are no rifts and no elites, only bosses and the swarm between them. It unlocks once you have defeated the Warden, right under the Daily Run on the title screen.
- **Race your splits.** The Gauntlet times every fight like a speedrun. A ladder at the top of the screen tracks the four bosses, a live split under the boss bar glows green while you are ahead of your best and red when you fall behind, and every boss you fell flashes its time and the difference from your record. Fell a boss in under 1:15 for a speed bonus. The Gauntlet screen keeps your best split for each boss, your fastest clear, your sum of best and your most bosses in one run.
- **Three new missions.** Gauntlet Runner (clear the Gauntlet), Lightspeed (clear it in under 4:00) and Eternal Return (defeat 8 bosses in one Gauntlet), worth 1,400 Flux together.
- **New sounds.** A gong as each boss steps into the Gauntlet, a stopwatch chime for every split (a brighter one for a new best) and a fanfare when the fourth boss falls.

## Oct 20, 2026 · 07:00 UTC

- **A new boss: the Leviathan.** At 2:00 a serpent of living light slithers out of the dark, a head trailed by twenty-six burning scales. Only its head can be hurt, and its body burns whatever it touches. It hunts where you are going, coils around you while its scales fire inward (slip out through the gap before the ring closes), then rears back, flashes white and lunges along a warning line. Wounded, it sheds its tail in a burst of shards at 75%, 50% and 25%. Kill it and its body bursts scale by scale into light and gems, leaving your first relic cache and a core. Take longer than a minute and it escapes with its prize.
- **A deeper Afterdark.** The Leviathan joins the night bosses, so the nights now rotate through the Warden, the Leviathan, the Seraph and the Eclipse.
- **Light trails.** Every ship now streams a ribbon of light, and it grows as you master the ship: an Ember at first, a Comet once that ship reaches the dawn, a Starwake that sheds stars at Radiance 3, a braided Helix at Radiance 6 and a Prism of every colour at Radiance 10. The Hangar shows each ship's trail and what earns the next one.
- **Two new missions.** Serpentfall (defeat the Leviathan) and Snake Charmer (defeat it without ever being touched). Nightmare now asks for six bosses in one run, and the Short Night mutator brings the Leviathan in at 1:30.
- **Boss entrances.** Every boss now arrives with a title card: the screen letterboxes, time slows, and its name and epithet burn in, from the Leviathan, Serpent of the Deep Dark, to the Eclipse, the Last Dark Before Dawn. The Leviathan brings its own roar, coiling hiss, lunge and shattering scales, and a Codex entry.

## Oct 20, 2026 · 03:00 UTC

- **New weapon: the Ricochet Glaive.** Throw a spinning glaive that leaps from enemy to enemy with a bright ricochet, then flies home through everything in its way. It is thrown again only once it is back in your hand, so fighting close to the swarm means faster throws. Levels add ricochets and more glaives, up to three at once.
- **New evolution: Ouroboros.** A maxed Ricochet Glaive with Renewal becomes four golden serpent glaives that grow 20% stronger with every ricochet (up to triple damage) and leap straight back out the moment they return. Nine evolutions now fill the Codex.
- **New ship: Echo, the resonance.** Starts with the Ricochet Glaive. Its Chain fades twice as slowly, and every 25 kills in it add 4% damage, up to 40%. Its Overdrive, Encore, throws a ring of glaives, and for six seconds every kill throws another and no hit can break your Chain. Unlocked by the Unbroken mission (a 300 Chain); if you already earned it, Echo is waiting in the Hangar.
- **New rift: Starfall.** From 1:30, a ring of cold light can open in the arena. Step inside and meteors start to fall, each marked by a closing target and a comet streaking in from above. Stay in the ring for 12 seconds without being struck and the last star falls as a Star Core (an instant level), with gems and repairs. The meteors crush the swarm too.
- **Two new missions.** Stargazer (weather a Starfall) and Ricochet (land 40 ricochets with one throw, which takes an Ouroboros). Grand Codex now asks for all nine evolutions.

## Oct 20, 2026 · 01:00 UTC

- **Rifts.** About once a minute a rift tears open somewhere in the arena, with an arrow pointing the way and a tracker under your loadout. Each one waits only so long, so you decide whether the detour is worth it. Every rift you complete adds 750 to your score.
- **Light Beacons, Prism Wisps and Umbral Trials.** Hold your ground inside a Beacon's ring while the swarm closes in, and it blazes into a Prism Cache and a shockwave. Chase down a Wisp that flees and sheds gems as it runs (it tires every few seconds), and it fills your Overdrive, repairs your hull and bursts into light. Touch an Umbral Rift and it releases a ring of the swarm: destroy enough of it before the timer runs out to win the trial.
- **Umbral relics.** A won trial offers three of five new relics that give great power at a price: Shattered Sun (+50% damage, a weaker hull), Hunger (+40% experience, a tougher swarm), Frenzy Engine (faster weapons, harder hits), Gambler's Prism (bigger crits, weaker everything else) and Chain Crown (a Chain multiplier up to x6, but a broken Chain hurts). Not tempted? Refuse all three and take a Prism Cache instead.
- **Three new missions and a bigger Codex.** Riftwalker (3 rifts in one run), Pathfinder (40 rifts in total) and Umbral Pact (hold 2 Umbral relics at once). The Codex now lists every rift and Umbral relic.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 22:00 UTC

- **Radiance.** Reach the dawn once and Radiance unlocks: ten levels of a brighter, harder night, chosen in the Hangar. Each level adds a rule on top of the last (Kindled, Restless, Hunted, Sharp, Titans, Scarcity, Swift, Legion, Impatient and Eventide) and raises your score by 15% and your Flux by 10%. Reach the dawn at your highest level to unlock the next one.
- **Ship mastery.** Every ship remembers the highest Radiance it has carried to the dawn, shown in the Hangar. Three new missions: Kindled (Radiance 1), Zenith (Radiance 5) and Eventide (Radiance 10), worth up to 1,500 Flux.
- **Banish.** Not the card you need? Tap BANISH on the level-up screen, then a card: it is gone for the rest of the run and a fresh card slides into its place. Every run gets one banish, and a new Forge upgrade adds two more.
- **Hits you can feel.** Taking damage now splits the screen into red and blue fringes with a jolt, Overdrives and boss kills punch a burst of radial blur out of your ship, and the edges shimmer when your hull runs low. Turning off Screen Shake turns these off too.
- **Coach tips.** The first time you meet an elite, a boss, a laser, the Chain, low hull, a charged Overdrive or a weapon ready to evolve, a short tip explains it, once.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 19:00 UTC

- **Relics.** The first elite you defeat and every boss now guard a relic: choose one of three, and it bends the rules for the rest of the run. Eight to find: Guardian Halo blocks a hit every 15 seconds (and saves your Chain), Ion Wake leaves a trail of burning light behind you, Starbreaker, Supercritical, Overcharge, Phoenix Plume, Midas Core and Wrath of Dawn.
- **New weapon: Ion Scythe.** A crescent of light sweeps through everything in front of you, and from level 4 behind you too. It rewards flying close to the swarm.
- **New weapon: Photon Mines.** Drop mines as you fly and lead the swarm over them. Up to eight wait on the field at once, and a full field sets off the oldest.
- **Two new evolutions.** Ion Scythe with Vitality Core becomes the **Lunar Reaper**, two full-circle sweeps that heal you with every kill. Photon Mines with Attractor becomes the **Constellation**, mines that link into burning tripwires and pull the swarm in. The Codex now lists eight evolutions and every relic.
- **A grand entrance, and new missions.** Every run opens with your ship warping in on a pillar of light. New missions: Reliquary (hold 4 relics in one run) and Grand Codex (discover all 8 evolutions).

## Oct 19, 2026 · 18:00 UTC

- **The Seraph.** A third boss now descends at 6:00. It spins a wheel of burning lasers you must ride the gaps of, fires lances aimed where you are going, then vanishes and reappears beside you in a ring of fire. Wounded, it grows a fourth arm and fires three lances at once. Defeat it for a boss cache, a core and a heart.
- **Three new enemies.** Phantoms fade out and blink into a ring of light beside you. Sentinels anchor, track you with a warning line and fire a long beam: step out of the line. Bastions hang back behind the swarm and halve the damage taken by everything inside their blue aegis, so break them first. Watch for the new Phantom ring at 2:30 and the Laser Grid at 3:25.
- **The Chain.** Every kill in a row builds your Chain, and every 25 kills raise its score multiplier, up to x4. Stop killing for a few seconds and it fades; take a hit and it shatters. Milestones from On Fire to Afterglow light up the screen, and your best Chain is recorded.
- **A deeper Afterdark.** Night bosses now rotate through the Warden, the Seraph and the Eclipse, and the swarm grows denser and tougher with every night you survive.
- **New missions.** Seraphfall, Unbroken (a 300 Chain) and Radiant (a 1,000 Chain). Nightmare now asks for five bosses in one run. The results screen shows your elites and best Chain, and the Codex lists every new foe.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 12:00 UTC

- **The Daily Run.** One hand-picked run per day, the same for every pilot: a fixed ship (even one you have not unlocked yet), one boon and two banes, and no Forge upgrades. Your first flight each day pays a Daily Bounty, keeping a streak raises it, and a log tracks your best score for every day.
- **Eleven mutators.** Volatile enemies that detonate, Lucky Star, Overcharged, Gravity, Gilded elite hunts, Glass Heart, Horde, Frenzy, Giants, Bloodlight and Short Night. Each bane raises the score multiplier, and each one has its own neon icon.
- **Into the Afterdark.** Defeat the Eclipse and you can now fly on. The arena bleeds crimson, the swarm keeps growing, and every 90 seconds the Warden or the Eclipse returns, stronger each night. Your dawn is banked the moment you choose; everything after it is score.
- **Score.** Every run now has a score built from kills, elites, bosses, time and the dawn. It ticks up live in the corner, counts up on the results screen and records your best overall and with every ship, shown in the Hangar.
- **The Codex and five new missions.** A new Codex screen shows all six evolutions (silhouettes until you discover them, recipes always visible) and a field guide to the swarm. New missions: Daybreaker, Ritual, Luminary, Into the Afterdark and Nightmare.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 09:00 UTC

- **The Hangar: seven ships.** Lumen is joined by Vesper the bulwark, Kestrel the hunter, Strix the glass cannon, Halcyon the harvester, Aurora the prism and Nyx the eclipse-born. Each one starts with its own weapon, plays by its own rules and has its own neon silhouette.
- **Overdrive.** Kills charge a ring in the corner of the screen. When it blazes, tap it to unleash your ship's signature power: Flare, Aegis, Barrage, Tempest, Black Sun, Prism Storm or Eclipse, which stops time itself. Space or E also work on a keyboard.
- **Missions.** Sixteen goals to chase, from surviving two minutes to reaching the dawn with three different ships. Every mission pays Flux, and six of them unlock new ships. Records from your earlier runs already count.
- **Nyx, the hard mode.** Fly Nyx and the swarm gets 40% tougher, but you earn 50% more experience and Flux. Reaching the dawn with Nyx pays the biggest bounty in the game.
- **A richer results screen.** Completed missions and newly unlocked ships now appear when a run ends, with a shortcut straight to the Hangar.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 06:00 UTC

- **Weapon Evolutions.** Max out a weapon, carry its catalyst system, then open a Prism Cache to evolve it into Supernova Cannon, Event Horizon, Storm Crown, Singularity, Hydra Swarm or Spectrum. Level-up cards show what evolves with what, and a ready weapon pulses gold in your loadout.
- **Prism Caches.** Elites and bosses now drop glowing caches under a pillar of light, with an arrow on the screen edge when one is off-screen. Opening one stops time for a slot-machine reveal of upgrades, evolutions and Flux.
- **Elite hunts.** Elites now roam in every minute or so after the third minute, so there is always a cache worth chasing.
- **Bosses fight back.** The Warden and the Eclipse are tougher, and they overload if you stall them for too long.
- **A fiercer late game.** The swarm grows tougher and denser after the fifth minute to match your new firepower. Your evolutions are recorded on the title screen and in the results.

## Oct 19, 2026 · 04:00 UTC

- **Welcome to Afterglow.** A brand-new game: pilot a ship of light through a neon arena while the swarm closes in. Drag anywhere to move; your weapons fire on their own.
- **Six weapons, ten systems.** Pulse Cannon, Halo Blades, Arc Coil, Nova Ring, Seeker Swarm and Prism Lance, each with five levels. Pick one of three upgrade cards every time you level up.
- **Eight enemy types and two bosses.** Dashing darts, splitting pentagons, orb-spitting stars, fused bombers, elites with golden halos, the Warden at 4:00 and the Eclipse at 8:00. Defeat the Eclipse to reach the dawn.
- **The Forge.** Every run earns Flux. Spend it on permanent upgrades: hull, power, engine, reroll cards and a once-per-run revive.
- **Built to glow.** Real bloom, a grid that ripples under every explosion, screen shake, slow motion on big kills, haptics and an adaptive synthwave soundtrack that heats up with the fight.
